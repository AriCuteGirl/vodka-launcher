# Roadmap

Phases 1 to 5 (the launcher, accounts and instances, performance, social and marketplace) are in. This is what comes
next, one phase at a time. Each item is finished, tested and committed before the next starts.

## Phase 6: Quality of life

- [ ] **6.1 Luduvo update progress.** When Luduvo updates itself or downloads its content before a game, show a
      progress bar instead of minutes of nothing (read from Luduvo's own launcher log).
- [ ] **6.2 Friend notifications.** A small pop-up when a friend comes online or starts a game, with Join. Roblox and
      Luduvo. Can be turned off.
- [ ] **6.3 Luduvo Play on Windows.** Needs where Luduvo installs on Windows (ask a tester).
- [ ] **6.4 Release 0.3.0** (Linux AppImage) and **Windows preview 4**.

## Phase 7: While you play

- [ ] **7.1 Second-screen HUD.** A small always-on-top window: session time, FPS and memory, friends' activity.
- [ ] **7.2 Discord Rich Presence.** "Playing *game* in Vodka" on your Discord profile. Opt-in.

## Phase 8: Trades

- [ ] **8.1 Roblox trades, read-only.** Inbound, outbound, completed, with item pictures and values.
- [ ] **8.2 Trade actions.** Accept, decline, counter, send: always behind a confirmation that shows both sides.
- [ ] **8.3 Luduvo trades, read-only.**

## Phase 9: Getting Vodka

- [ ] **9.1 Update notice and self-update** from GitHub releases.
- [ ] **9.2 Flatpak and AUR packages.**

## Phase 10: Luduvo extras

- [ ] **10.1 Groups page** (yours, browse, join).
- [ ] **10.2 Several Luduvo accounts**, like Roblox's Players page.
- [ ] **10.3 Body colors** in the Avatar page, once Luduvo's call for saving them is known.

Not planned: Luduvo chat (not needed), in-game input automation (against Roblox's rules).
